- Right side of the screen (~50% of it):

  - Small mini map on the bottom right, can be toggled on and off, semi-transparent until mouse hovers over, when mouse hovers over, can click to teleport to an area
- Left side of the screen (other ~50% of it):
  - Bottom bit of the screen: tab switching, can also switch tabs with a keybind
  - Tab 1: Animation, timeline below it, current rendering stats (like current render FPS, timing controls, etc.)
  - Tab 2: Nodes, drag and drop type thing, multiple tabs on the left for different groups (ex. All, Basics, IO, etc.)
  - Tab 3: Debug, debug logs and node heat mpa (the node minimap but instead it shows what's taking the longest to process/calculate)
  - Tab 4: Export, export settings and export button
  - Tab 5: Settings, settings (theme and maybe keybinds), credits, any funny messages
